Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 27, 2026

Overview

TikTok Tracker is a personal analytics tool used to view statistics for TikTok content associated with an account authorized by its owner. Access occurs only after the user gives explicit authorization through TikTok OAuth.

Information the application may access

After authorization, TikTok Tracker may access TikTok profile and video information made available for the authorized account through the official TikTok API. This may include public video statistics such as view, like, comment, and share counts.

How information is used

The authorized information is used only to provide personal video-statistics analysis. The specific local statistics application may process this data as needed for that analysis. This public website does not make claims about storage behavior that has not been implemented or configured in the local application.

Data sharing and sale

TikTok Tracker does not sell personal data. Authorized TikTok information is not accessed for advertising purposes.

Website tracking and cookies

This public website does not use analytics, tracking technologies, advertising cookies, or other advertising tools. It consists only of static informational pages.

Revoking access

Users may end the application's future access by revoking TikTok authorization through the controls provided by TikTok. Once authorization is revoked, the application can no longer request authorized account data unless access is granted again.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if the application's functionality or data practices change. The date at the top of this page identifies the current version.
